Meet Your Therapist

A Grounded, Compassionate Space for Your Metamorphosis

“Healing is not about becoming someone new—it is about unburdening who you are and returning to your grounded center.”

Welcome. I am Jessica Bravo, a Licensed Clinical Social Worker (NC License #C017999) providing dedicated, trauma-informed teletherapy across all of North Carolina. My practice is built on creating a secure, culturally affirming sanctuary where you can explore your thoughts without judgment.

As a bilingual clinician (English & Spanish), I specialize in helping individuals and couples untangle generational patterns, heal attachment wounds, and navigate monumental life transitions. My therapeutic work thoughtfully blends Internal Family Systems (IFS), Somatic Experiencing, and Relational Psychodynamics to facilitate lasting, rooted transformation.

Grounded Healing

Somatic body awareness to restore emotional safety and nervous system regulation.

Transformational Care

Internal Family Systems (IFS) to harmonize inner parts, release trauma, and step into self-energy.

Culturally Inclusive

Bilingual (EN/ES), culturally attuned care honoring heritage, generational resilience, and lived experience.
