What is IFS?

IFS is an evidence-based therapeutic modality used to identify the root causes behind emotional and behavioral patterns. One of the core concepts of IFS is the Self, which refers to the core compassionate center of every person. Self is equipped to lead and heal. The term Self energy can be used interchangeably with Soul energy.

Understanding Your Parts

IFS understands that we are made up of various parts. All parts have a role within our internal system and carry their own history. Through IFS we develop compassionate relationships with all of our parts. The focus of IFS is to access our Self energy and help our parts heal the burdens they carry.

IFS is a transformative, trauma-informed, evidence-based, holistic approach.
